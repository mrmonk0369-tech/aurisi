How selling works
1. Upload your lecture videos to your YouTube channel as Unlisted and add the links above - students only ever see them inside the Aurisi app.
2. Generate access codes above. Each code unlocks this course for one student.
3. Share your UPI ID with students: not set - after a student pays you directly, send them one code.
4. Students open the Aurisi app, enter the code, and your course unlocks with all videos - branded, clean, inside your institute's Aurisi experience.
